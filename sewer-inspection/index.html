Sewer Inspection in West Hollywood, CA

A sewer camera inspection answers the question a cable cannot: what is actually wrong inside the pipe. In West Hollywood, CA, where many buildings are older and laterals are private, a short video can save a wrong repair, support a purchase decision or settle who is responsible.

This page explains what the camera shows, what a locator adds, and when an inspection is worth booking before a clog, a sale or a remodel.

What the camera shows

A waterproof camera on a flexible rod travels through a cleanout or a fixture opening. It shows the pipe material, joint condition, cracks, offsets, roots, sags where water collects, grease and scale, and any collapse. You get the footage and a plain-language summary with the depth and location of each finding.

We note the footage position so a repair can be targeted. If the line is blocked, it may need clearing first so the camera can pass, which is why an inspection often follows a cleaning.

When an inspection is worth booking

These situations make the most of it.

  • Repeat clogsThe cause is probably in the pipe, not the fixture.
  • Buying a building or houseThe seller report may not include the lateral. An inspection before closing can protect the buyer.
  • Before a remodelKnow the condition of the line before new fixtures go on it.
  • Sewer smell with no clogA cracked line can leak gas without backing up.
  • After tree work or nearby constructionRoots or ground movement may have damaged a joint.
  • Insurance or property management questionsFootage documents the condition.

Locating the line and depth

A locator receives the signal from the camera head and marks its position at the surface. We mark the path and depth on the ground or on a sketch so a repair crew knows exactly where to dig, or whether a trenchless method makes more sense.

In West Hollywood, with 1.89 square miles of land and a dense street grid, a lateral often runs a short distance to the main. That makes accurate marking important, because the line may cross a driveway, a courtyard or a property boundary.

Private lateral, city main and what the footage settles

The City of West Hollywood cleans its own sewer mainlines, and owners maintain the private lateral, including the connection to the main. The city also advises owners to have the lateral video inspected and cleaned routinely as needed.

Footage that shows a problem past the property line helps you hand the issue to the city. Footage that shows a problem on your side helps you plan a repair with the right license. Work on the private side can be done by a C-36 plumber, while work in the public right-of-way needs a permit and a different license class.
