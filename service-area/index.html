Where we work

Our home base is West Hollywood, ZIP code 90069, on Santa Monica Boulevard. Three nearby areas have their own pages: Beverly Hills to the west, the Hollywood Hills to the north and Hollywood to the east.

Each page explains the utility, the pressure and the building types in that area, because they differ. Beverly Hills runs its own water department, the hills bring steep access and the City of Los Angeles sewer rules, and Hollywood means older apartments and busy commercial streets.

Neighborhoods nearby

Beyond those pages, we reach Beverly Grove and the Fairfax District to the south, Studio City over the ridge and Culver City for scheduled work. Timing depends on traffic and crew location, so we confirm it by phone before promising a window.

If your address is on a private road, behind a gate or in a hillside lot, mention it when you call. A truck needs a place to park, and we plan for it.

Calls from these neighborhoods follow the same steps as any other. The dispatcher confirms the address, tells you what to do while you wait and gives you the earliest window the crew can meet. If the timing is longer than you can wait, we tell you so, and you can decide whether to book us or call another plumber closer to you.

Streets and neighborhoods inside West Hollywood

West Hollywood covers 1.89 square miles of land, and the plumbing differs from block to block. The Sunset Strip and the blocks around Santa Monica Boulevard and La Cienega Boulevard mix restaurants, clubs and apartment buildings. Norma Triangle, West Hollywood West, East and North are residential, and many of their buildings are older.

Landmarks such as Plummer Park, the Pacific Design Center and the Schindler House are part of how callers describe where they are. Give the dispatcher a cross street as well, since many buildings sit close together.

Why the line between cities matters

A plumbing job in West Hollywood follows the West Hollywood Plumbing Code, which requires a permit for installation, alteration, repair, replacement or remodel of a plumbing system and exempts leak repairs and clearing stoppages. A job in the Hollywood Hills or Hollywood follows the City of Los Angeles rules, and a job in Beverly Hills follows that city.

We tell you which applies to your address, and we say plainly when a rule is outside what we handle. That is part of why each area has its own page.

Hard-to-reach addresses

Some addresses take more planning. Hillside streets can be narrow, and a truck may need a place to stop that is not in front of the house. Gated estates need a code or a contact. Large buildings may need a manager to meet us.

Tell the dispatcher about any of these when you call at (213) 672-5590. We would rather solve the access question on the phone than discover it on the street at midnight.

How to confirm coverage

Call the line and give the dispatcher your street address. We tell you plainly whether we can reach you and how soon. If we cannot, we say so rather than offering a window we cannot meet.
