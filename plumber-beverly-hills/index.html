Beverly Hills water: its own utility, chloramine and hardness

Beverly Hills operates its own water department, and the city lists emergency water shutoff and sewer stoppage service on its Public Works pages. Most of West Hollywood is served by LADWP, so a Beverly Hills address has a different utility, a different bill and a different customer service line.

The city reports water hardness of about 290 mg/L, which is roughly 17 grains per gallon, and it disinfects with chloramine. Hard water leaves scale in water heaters, on fixtures and inside pipes. We do not blame hardness for any single failure, but we do check for scale when we open a heater or a valve, and we mention it when a softener or filter would save you trouble later.

Sewer and drain calls in older Beverly Hills neighborhoods

Mature trees line many Beverly Hills streets, and roots find clay and cast iron laterals at the joints. A camera run shows whether you have root entry, a belly where water pools or a joint that has shifted. We use that footage to decide between cleaning, spot repair and replacement.

Private laterals are the owner's responsibility in most California cities, and Beverly Hills Public Works handles the city main. If the camera shows the problem past your property line, we tell you so you can call the city. See sewer line repair for how repair choices compare.
