Leaky Toilet at the Base in West Hollywood: Wax Rings, Bolts and Flanges

Water pooling around the base of a toilet gets blamed on the wax ring, and often it is the wax ring. Not always. A loose bolt, a supply line drip, a sweating tank or a cracked bowl can all leave the same puddle.

The steps below sort those causes in a sensible order. The first test costs nothing, and it keeps you from replacing a seal that was fine.

Dry everything and watch

Wipe the base, the tank, the supply line and the floor completely dry. Put a few sheets of dry toilet paper around the base. Flush and watch. Paper that turns wet right after a flush points at the seal under the bowl. Paper that stays dry until later points at the tank, the supply line or condensation.

Do this with the supply line connection too, because a drip from the shutoff valve runs down the line and ends up at the base.

Causes of a wet base

Several problems look the same on the floor.

Why wax rings fail

A wax ring is compressed between the bottom of the toilet and the flange. If the toilet rocks, the seal breaks, and every flush leaks a little. A flange that sits too low, a toilet that was set unevenly or a floor that has settled also breaks the seal.

Sewer gas can leak through a failed seal before water shows. A faint smell near the toilet, with no clog, is another sign to look at the ring.

The reset, step by step

It is a hands-on job that needs a helper and a tolerance for the unpleasant.

  1. 1
    Close the valve and empty the toiletFlush and sponge out the water.
  2. 2
    Remove the toiletDisconnect the supply, take off the nuts and lift straight up.
  3. 3
    Clean and check the flangeScrape off the old wax. Look for cracks, rust or a low flange.
  4. 4
    Set a new seal and the toiletLower it straight down, press evenly and add washers and nuts.
  5. 5
    Tighten, caulk the base and testTighten in turns. Test with several flushes.

Damage under the toilet

A long-term leak can soak the subfloor and rot the wood around the flange. Press a screwdriver into the floor beside the toilet. If it sinks in, the floor is damaged. A flange fastened to rotten wood will not hold, and the same leak returns after a month.

In an upstairs bathroom, a leak at the base can stain the ceiling below. That is a reason to treat a wet base as urgent. The leak detection page explains how we confirm whether water is coming from the toilet or from another source.

Do not do these

They make the problem worse.

  • Do not keep flushing a rocking toiletEach flush pushes water past the broken seal.
  • Do not caulk the base to hide a leakIt traps water under the bowl and hides rot.
  • Do not overtighten the boltsPorcelain cracks under uneven pressure.
  • Do not ignore a sewage smellIt can mean the seal is already open.

Does the toilet need replacing instead?

A reset with a new ring suits a toilet in good shape. A toilet with a hairline crack, a worn glaze that holds stains or a tank that has been repaired several times is a poor candidate. Spending money on a worn fixture only delays the replacement.

Look at the floor too. If the flange is badly corroded or the subfloor is soft, replace those first. A new toilet on a bad floor fails in the same way as the old one, and the repair bill then includes the toilet twice.

After the repair: how to know it held

Dry the floor and place a fresh sheet of toilet paper around the base. Flush three or four times over the next day and check the paper each time. A dry sheet means the seal is holding.

Check again after a week, since a seal that is slightly off can seep slowly. Look for a gap between the toilet and the floor, and press on each side of the bowl to confirm it does not move. A toilet that shifts again needs the flange checked.

Keep the receipts and a note of the date. If you rent, send your manager a short message that the repair is done, so the record is complete.

What West Hollywood buildings change here

The city has a high share of multi-unit buildings, and the 2020 census counted 35,757 residents in 1.89 square miles. In a dense building a leak at one base can reach the ceiling of the unit below within a day. Tell your manager promptly and keep your own notes with dates.

West Hollywood rent stabilization covers properties with an original certificate of occupancy before July 1, 1979.

Many of them have older flanges and cast iron drains where the bottom of the toilet meets the pipe, and a recurring problem can also point to a partly blocked branch line, which is a drain cleaning job. If the flange is cast iron and corroded, the repair is more than a seal swap. Our Hollywood and Beverly Hills customers see the same pattern in older buildings.
