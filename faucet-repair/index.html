Emergency Faucet Repair in West Hollywood, CA

A faucet that will not shut off, or a supply line that has burst, is an emergency. We take faucet repair calls in West Hollywood, CA 24/7. While you wait, close the shutoff valves under the sink by turning them clockwise, and keep towels in the cabinet.

Stopping water from a faucet emergency

Under the sink there are two small valves, one for hot and one for cold. Turn each clockwise until it stops. If a valve will not turn, or if it leaks when you touch it, close the unit shutoff instead and tell us.

A burst braided supply line is the most common sink flood in a rental. It sprays steadily and can empty into the cabinet and the unit below within minutes. Shut the valve, mop and call.

Drips, hard water and mineral buildup

Beverly Hills Water reports hardness near 290 mg/L, about 17 grains per gallon, and LADWP hardness varies by source. Hard water leaves scale on aerators and inside cartridges, which stiffens handles and causes drips. Cleaning the aerator can restore flow, and a worn cartridge needs replacing.

If scale builds repeatedly, a softener or filter reduces it for the whole building. For a single faucet, we recommend simple cleaning and a quality cartridge.

Repair or replace

Repairing a good faucet is usually less costly and faster. A pitted finish, a corroded body, a cracked spout or parts no longer made are reasons to replace. We carry common brands and can install a faucet you provide.

New faucets come with their own supply lines. We replace the old braided lines and check the shutoff valves while we are there, because a worn valve is a small job today and a flood later.

Landlords, tenants and the quick fix

West Hollywood is a majority-renter city, and many leaks start in aging kitchens and baths. Rent-stabilized buildings with certificates of occupancy before July 1, 1979 often have older fixtures. Tell your manager in writing about a leak, because an unreported drip becomes a damaged cabinet.

If you are the owner or manager, a faucet kit and an annual valve check keep small leaks small. We can recommend what to stock for your building.

Signs a faucet needs attention

Do not wait for a flood.

  • Drip that does not stop when closedWorn seat or cartridge.
  • Handle that is loose or stiffWorn parts or scale buildup.
  • Water under the sinkA loose connection or a failing line.
  • Low flow from one faucetA clogged aerator or cartridge.

What to do before we arrive

A few steps protect the cabinet and the floor.

  • Close both shutoff valves under the sinkTurn each one clockwise until it stops.
  • Clear the cabinetMove cleaning supplies out so we can reach the connections.
  • Do not keep running the tapA faucet that is leaking inside the body gets worse with use.
  • Note the brand and any model numberIt is often on the underside of the body or in the manual.
