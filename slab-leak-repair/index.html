Does your building have a slab?

Slab foundations are common in single-story construction and in ground-floor units. Hillside houses and many older multi-story buildings use raised floors or garages below, so water lines run through walls and ceilings instead. If your supply lines enter through the floor, a slab leak is possible. If they enter from above or through a crawlspace, the leak is probably elsewhere.

A camera and leak detection visit settles it. We do not sell a slab repair unless the leak is actually under the concrete.

Slab leak emergency: symptoms to act on

Warm spots on the floor point to a hot water line leaking beneath. A constantly running meter, the sound of water with all fixtures closed, damp carpet or discoloration at baseboards and falling water pressure are other signs. Mineral stains or cracked tile can follow when moisture rises to the surface.

These signs can also come from a leak in a wall or a failed fixture. That is why detection comes before any decision to cut concrete.

Why West Hollywood water chemistry matters

Beverly Hills Water reports hardness of about 290 mg/L, which is roughly 17 grains per gallon, for its supply. LADWP reports hardness that varies by source across its system. Hard water leaves mineral scale and can speed pitting in copper over decades, and fast flow through a pipe at a bend can wear it from inside.

We do not claim hardness caused any particular leak. We do check the pipe condition when we open it, and we tell you what we see. Where scale is heavy, a softener or filtration system is a long-term conversation.

Permits and the concrete question

The West Hollywood Plumbing Code requires a permit for replacement of water piping, while simple leak repairs on water piping, valves or fixtures are exempt. Rerouting or repiping falls on the permit side. We tell you before the work begins which category your job falls in and who pulls the permit.

Concrete work leaves a patch. We explain how the floor will be restored and whether flooring above needs to be removed, so the surprise is not on your side.
