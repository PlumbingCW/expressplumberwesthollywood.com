Who we are

West Hollywood Express Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 8714 Santa Monica Boulevard in West Hollywood, California. We take emergency and scheduled calls for homes, apartments and small businesses in West Hollywood, Beverly Hills, the Hollywood Hills and Hollywood.

We keep the story simple. The phone is answered by a person at any hour, the crew arrives with the equipment you described, and you hear the price before work begins.

What we do and what we do not do

We handle drains, sewer lines, leaks, water heaters, fixtures, repiping, installations and commercial plumbing. Our drain cleaning and water heater repair pages are the busiest.

We do not do gas line work, and we do not provide water damage restoration or mold work. When a job needs another trade, such as a restoration crew or an electrician, we tell you and coordinate where it helps.

How we handle licensing and trust

We would rather you check us than take our word for it.

  • Verify any contractorCalifornia requires a licensed contractor for jobs above a set amount of labor and materials. Check a license at cslb.ca.gov before you hire anyone, including us.
  • Deposits and cancellationState rules cap a down payment at 10 percent of the contract price or a fixed state limit, whichever is less, and give you three days to cancel certain home improvement contracts.
  • Written findingsAfter a camera visit or a leak search, you receive the cause and the work in writing.
  • Honest limitsIf a repair is not worth the money, we say so.

How we keep prices clear

Plumbing prices depend on what we find, so we do not quote a number before we know the problem. What we do is explain the factors that will shape it, such as access, parts, equipment and time of day, and then give you the price in plain terms before any work starts.

If the camera or the leak search shows more than you expected, we stop, show you what we found and ask what you want to do. Nothing is added without your word, and you can decline and still keep the written findings.

How dispatch works

The number on this site rings the same dispatch line at noon and at 3 a.m. We ask for your address, what is happening and whether anyone else is affected, and we book the earliest appointment available. During the call we tell you what to do while the crew is on the way.

We list our service area as West Hollywood and the towns next door. If your address is outside that area, we say so on the phone rather than promising a window we cannot meet. See the service area page for the list.

Who calls us

Most of our calls come from three kinds of customers. Renters in multi-unit buildings call about leaks, backups and no hot water, and they often need us to coordinate with a manager. Owners of houses and condos call about water heaters, drains and older pipe. Small businesses along the main streets call about restrooms, grease lines and water heaters.

Each group has different questions. A renter wants to know what to do before the owner answers. An owner wants to know what a repair will cost and whether a permit is needed. A manager wants a written note of the cause. We try to answer all three on the first call.

What a first call usually covers

The same few questions come up, whatever the problem.

What is happening and where?It decides the crew and the equipment on the truck.
Can you shut off the water?Stopping the flow limits damage before we arrive.
Who else is affected?Shared stacks and shared heaters involve the manager.

What to expect from a visit

A visit follows the same pattern, whether it is a clog or a repair.

  • Arrival and a short walk-throughWe look at the problem and ask what you have noticed.
  • A clear priceYou hear it before work starts. If the job changes, we stop and tell you.
  • The work and a testWe fix the problem and run the fixture or line to confirm.
  • A short written noteIt lists the cause, the work and anything to watch for.
