When no hot water is an emergency

Some outages can wait until the morning. These cannot.

  • Water at the base of the tankShut the cold supply to the heater and call. Do not wait to see whether it dries.
  • A gas smell near the closet or garageLeave the building and call 911 or the gas utility before you call us. A gas leak is never a plumbing appointment to schedule.
  • Hot water that is scalding or steaming from tapsA stuck thermostat or a failed valve can push the water well past a safe setting.
  • A discharge pipe running continuouslyThe temperature and pressure relief valve is releasing for a reason, and the cause needs finding the same day.
  • An older tank in an upper-floor unitAny leak above a neighbor is time-sensitive, even if it looks small.

What actually fails on a gas water heater

On a gas tank, the three common faults are a dirty or failed pilot, a thermocouple or flame sensor that no longer proves the flame, and a gas control valve that has worn out. A pilot that lights and then drops out when you release the button points at the thermocouple. A burner that will not stay lit under load often traces to a partly blocked burner tube or a draft problem in the vent.

Sediment is the other long-term issue. Minerals settle on the tank floor and the burner has to heat through them, which causes popping noises and slower recovery. Flushing helps when the tank is young. On an older tank, a heavy sediment layer often means the lining is near the end.

Electric tanks: elements, thermostats and breakers

An electric tank has two heating elements and two thermostats. If you get lukewarm water, the lower element has often burned out. If you get no hot water at all, the upper thermostat or the high-limit reset button is the first suspect. A breaker that trips again as soon as it is reset usually means an element has shorted to the tank and needs replacing, not resetting.

We test with a meter before changing a part, because swapping an element when the real fault is a thermostat wastes a visit. If the tank has already been leaking at the element gasket, the gasket and element are replaced together.

Hybrid, electric and gas units in older West Hollywood buildings

Buildings in West Hollywood that predate July 1979 fall under the city rent stabilization ordinance, and many were built long before efficient water heaters existed. In those buildings the heater often lives in a closet that was sized for a very different appliance. That affects venting, combustion air and how much clearance a new unit needs.

Heat pump and hybrid electric units are becoming common as owners look to cut gas use. They need space and airflow, and the repair path for them involves a compressor and refrigerant circuit that a standard tank repair does not. We tell you early if your unit is better served by its manufacturer or by replacement.

If the repair turns into a replacement

When the tank itself has failed, the answer is water heater replacement, and a unit that is short on capacity may be a candidate for a tankless upgrade. A hot water problem in a shared building often involves commercial plumbing skills too.
