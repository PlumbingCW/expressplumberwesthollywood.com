When a clogged drain is an emergency

A single slow sink is an inconvenience. Several fixtures backing up at once, a toilet that gurgles when the shower drains, or water rising in a tub when you flush are signs of a blockage in a shared or main line. That is an emergency, because sewage can come up through the lowest drain in the unit.

In a multi-unit West Hollywood building, a blockage in the stack or the building lateral can affect neighbors on the floors below you. If more than one unit is affected, tell the property manager at once and keep the plumber informed. If only your fixtures back up, the blockage is usually in your own branch line.

Our dispatch line answers around the clock. We ask which fixtures are affected, whether anything else drains badly, and whether you can see sewage, because the answers decide what equipment goes on the truck.

Kitchen sink clogs and what really causes them

Kitchen drains clog with grease, food solids, coffee grounds and starch. Grease cools in the pipe and builds a ring that narrows the bore, and each meal adds to it. A kitchen line in an older building has a long horizontal run, which is where grease settles.

A hand-crank cable removes the soft plug at the trap or the first few feet of line. When the clog is further down the branch, a longer cable or a hydro jet is used. Where a disposal sits ahead of the trap, we check that the disposal is not the actual blockage before cabling past it.

If your kitchen sink backs up into the second basin or the dishwasher air gap, the blockage is past the branch junction. That points at the kitchen line rather than the trap.

Bathroom drains: hair, soap scum and shower pans

Tub and shower drains clog with hair wound around the strainer arm or the stopper linkage, and with soap scum that glues it together. Pulling the stopper and clearing the first few inches often solves it. If the water level in the tub rises when the sink runs, the shared branch is blocked further down.

Sink drains behind a pedestal or vanity can clog at the trap, and a missing cleanout cap or a loose slip joint can leak when cabled. We protect finishes and check every joint after clearing. Toilets that will not flush normally are a separate path, covered under toilet repair.

Main line symptoms and who owns what

A main-line blockage shows up in the lowest fixtures first, often a ground-floor shower, a laundry standpipe or a floor drain. The City of West Hollywood maintains its sewer mainlines, and property owners are responsible for the private lateral that connects a building to the main, including the connection itself. The city says its crews respond to sewer problems around the clock.

If you can see that the city main is overflowing at a manhole in the street, call the city. If the blockage is inside your building or on the lateral, call us. A camera run tells which it is, and the line between the two matters for who pays and who can legally dig.

What not to do while you wait

A few habits make a clog worse.

  • Do not pour chemical drain cleanerIt can sit in the pipe, damage some pipe materials and creates a hazard for the person who opens the trap.
  • Do not keep flushing or running waterMore water means more overflow if the line is fully blocked.
  • Do not use a plunger on a fully backed-up multi-fixture linePressure can push sewage out of the next weakest fixture.
  • Do not pour grease down any drainIt looks liquid hot and sets inside the line as it cools.
