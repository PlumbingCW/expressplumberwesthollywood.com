After a Water Main Break: What West Hollywood Owners Should Do

In July 2026, a 36-inch LADWP trunk line under Sunset Boulevard at Holloway Drive ruptured. ABC7 reported that nearly 17 million gallons flowed down Palm Avenue, and the mayor estimated that 150 to 200 vehicles were damaged in flooded garages. A sinkhole opened, and two residential buildings lost power.

The break was on the utility side. Owners were still left with questions about damage, claims and their own plumbing. This guide describes what to do after a break like that.

Document first, clean second

Photograph and video the water, the damage and the dates before you move anything. Keep receipts for pumping, drying, repairs and any belongings you replace. LADWP directed affected customers to its claims page at ladwp.com/claims, and advised keeping photos, receipts and repair estimates.

For vehicles, check your auto policy for the coverage that applies to flood damage. A call to your insurer should come early and should be in writing.

Check your own plumbing afterward

A surge, a pressure swing or a loss of pressure can stress old fittings. After the water returns, check every fixture for leaks, look under sinks and test the water heater. A pressure test at a hose bib shows whether your regulator is working. The California Plumbing Code calls for a regulator when static pressure exceeds 80 psi.

LADWP also noted that some customers on higher floors saw low pressure during the event. If pressure stays low after service returns, the cause may be a partly closed valve or air in the lines. If it stays low, see water line repair.

Checklist for the first day

These steps limit secondary damage.

  • Shut your main valve if water is enteringKnow where it is before the next event.
  • Stay out of flooded garages with live powerElectricity and water do not mix.
  • Run each tap and flush each toiletClear air and check for leaks.
  • Check the water heaterSediment and air can disturb it.
  • Call your insurer and take photosEarly notice helps.

Why a backflow or sewer backup can follow

Heavy flooding can overload street drains and push water into building drains. If drains gurgle or back up, stop using water and call a plumber. A cleaning and camera look shows whether debris is in the line. See drain cleaning.

A backwater valve can protect low fixtures from backflow. It needs a permit in the City of Los Angeles, and in West Hollywood you should confirm the permit steps with the city. We can advise after a camera look.

What to expect from the utility and the city in the days after

Expect street work. A repaired trunk line is often followed by temporary paving, lane closures and a later permanent repair. The Sunset Boulevard section reopened with a temporary paving note, so plan for a second round of work and for detours on nearby streets.

Expect the utility to answer questions about service, pressure and claims, and the city to answer questions about streets, sidewalks and sinkholes. Neither agency answers for your private plumbing, so keep your own plumber and your own insurer in the loop. A short call to each, with your photos ready, saves a week of back and forth.

If you manage a building, send residents one clear message with the date service returned, the steps to flush taps and the number to call for a leak. Residents who know what to do call sooner, and sooner costs less.

Garages, power and safety first

Flooded garages are the most dangerous place after a break. Water can reach panels, outlets and car batteries. Stay out until the power to the area is off, and let an electrician decide when it is safe to go back. Two residential buildings near the Sunset Boulevard break lost power until electricians replaced panels.

Do not run a vacuum or a pump from a wet outlet. Use a ground-fault outlet on a dry circuit, or call a pumping service. Standing water can carry contaminants, so wear boots and gloves and wash your hands after.

Keep a simple log

Write down the time you saw water, the time it was shut off and the time service returned. Note who you spoke with at the utility and the claim number if you open one. These dates answer questions the insurer and the utility will ask.

A phone note works. Add photos of damaged items with the date visible. Keep estimates from repair shops, even if you do not use them, since LADWP advised owners to hold on to photos, receipts and estimates.

What this event means for West Hollywood and its neighbors

The Sunset Boulevard break reopened the road on July 24, 2026 at about 11 p.m., with a temporary paving note. LADWP said it is accelerating replacement of that trunk line, which had been scheduled for 2031. The break happened on a street that runs across West Hollywood, the Hollywood Hills and Beverly Hills.

The event is a reminder to learn your valve locations, photograph your garage and utility closet in normal condition and keep your insurance documents in one place.
