Tankless Water Heater in West Hollywood, CA

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows, so there is no tank to leak and no standby loss. In West Hollywood, CA, where closets are small and many buildings are older, that compactness is the main reason people choose one. The trade-off is that a tankless unit has tighter demands on gas sizing, venting and maintenance.

This page covers installation, repair and the yearly flush that keeps a unit working. If the unit is already failing and you have no hot water, call the line and we will triage it the same way we do a failed tank.

Install, repair or flush: three different jobs

Installation means sizing the unit to the fixtures you run at once, upgrading the gas line if needed, and venting through a rated pipe. Repair means reading the error code, then checking flow, gas supply, sensors and the heat exchanger. Flush and descale means circulating a descaling solution through the heat exchanger to remove mineral buildup.

Many people call after a service company says it does not repair tankless units. We repair many common faults, and when a unit is past economical repair we say so plainly.

Signs a tankless unit needs attention

These show up before a unit fails.

  • Error codes on the displayMost codes point to flow, ignition, venting or a temperature sensor. The code is the starting point, not the diagnosis.
  • Lukewarm water or temperature swingsScale on the heat exchanger and a clogged inlet screen are common causes.
  • Flow stops when you open a second tapThe unit may be undersized for simultaneous use, or the inlet screen is restricting flow.
  • Banging or kettle-like noiseScale inside the heat exchanger is boiling water in place.
  • Water that smells or tastes metallicA failing heat exchanger or old plumbing deserves inspection.

Why flushing matters with local water

West Hollywood water comes mainly from the Los Angeles Department of Water and Power, and Beverly Hills Water serves certain areas on the west side of the city. Beverly Hills reports hardness of about 290 mg/L, which is 17 grains per gallon. LADWP publishes hardness figures by source in its annual water quality tables, and the figures vary from one source to another.

Hard water leaves scale. In a tankless heater, scale forms right where heat transfer happens, so even a thin layer lowers output and raises temperature spikes. Descaling once a year is a common recommendation. If your water is on the hard end, ask about a filter or softener upstream of the unit.

How a descale flush is done

A flush takes a fraction of the time of a new install and extends the unit life.

  1. 1
    Isolate the unitClose the water and gas supply valves and attach flush valves to the service ports.
  2. 2
    Circulate the solutionA small pump circulates descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer specifies.
  3. 3
    Rinse and clean the screenFlush clean water through, then remove and clean the inlet filter screen.
  4. 4
    Restore and testReopen the valves, relight the unit and check the temperature rise and error history.

Tight spaces, older buildings and approvals

Many West Hollywood buildings were designed with a single closet for a tank heater. A tankless replacement can fit in that footprint, but the vent route and the gas supply change. The West Hollywood Plumbing Code treats installation and replacement as permit work, and gas in the city comes from Southern California Gas Company.

If your tank has failed and you want to move to tankless, see water heater installation for the planning steps, and emergency water heater replacement if you need hot water fast.

What affects tankless pricing

Installation, repair and flush are each quoted before we start.

Gas line size

A tankless unit draws a lot of gas at once, so a line sized for a tank may need upgrading.

Vent path

Direct vent through a wall or roof, with the right material.

Condensate drain

High-efficiency units produce acidic condensate that must be drained or neutralized.

Brand and model

Parts, service tools and software differ.

Water quality

Scale conditions change how often the unit needs service.

Insurance does not cover wear, scale or sensor faults. It may cover water damage from a supply leak if the cause was sudden.

Tankless care and the alternatives

A tankless unit that is not working is a water heater repair call. If you are weighing it against a tank, see water heater installation. Scale control through softener and filtration protects the heat exchanger.
