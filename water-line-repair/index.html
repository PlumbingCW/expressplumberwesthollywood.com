Water line emergency: where the utility ends and your line begins

LADWP serves most of West Hollywood, and Beverly Hills Water serves certain west side areas. In general the utility owns the main in the street and the meter, and the owner is responsible for the pipe from the meter into the building. The utility's own customer service can confirm where your boundary sits.

If you see water gushing from the street, call the utility. If the leak is between the meter and your building, it is ours to repair.

What the July 2026 Sunset Boulevard break taught residents

In July 2026 a 36-inch LADWP trunk line under Sunset Boulevard ruptured near Holloway Drive, sending millions of gallons down nearby streets and into garages. That break was on the utility side, but it left many owners asking what is theirs and what is not. LADWP also noted that some customers on higher floors saw low pressure during the event.

For you, the practical lesson is to know where your building shutoff is, to photograph anything that gets damaged and to keep receipts. Utility-side failures go through the utility claims process. Failures on your side go to your plumber and your insurer.

Pressure and the regulator

The California Plumbing Code calls for a pressure regulator when static pressure is above 80 psi. Many buildings in hilly neighborhoods get high pressure from the street, and a worn regulator lets it through. We test pressure at a hose bib and replace a regulator that is failing.

Low pressure is a different issue. It can come from a leak, a partly closed valve or a clogged line, and our test shows which. The same test helps with the leak detection question.

Signs your service line is leaking

Check these before calling.

  • Wet spot or soft groundWater is surfacing between the meter and the building.
  • Meter spins with fixtures offWater is escaping somewhere on your side.
  • Low pressure at every fixtureThe line may be restricted or leaking.
  • Sound of running water at the entryWater is escaping near the building.

What to do before we arrive

Basic steps limit damage until the line is repaired.

  • Close the main valve at the buildingIf you cannot reach it, tell the dispatcher.
  • Keep people away from soft or sunken groundWater can undermine soil and pavement.
  • Do not dig at the leak yourselfYou may hit another line or a cable.
  • Photograph the area and the meterA photo of the dial helps us and the utility.
