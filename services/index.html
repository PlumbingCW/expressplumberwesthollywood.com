Emergency or scheduled: how to tell

Emergency pages cover problems where waiting makes the damage worse: water that will not stop, sewage coming up, no hot water for a whole building or a leak behind a wall. Scheduled pages cover installs, upgrades and inspections where you can pick a time.

Many jobs start as one and end as the other. A camera inspection after a backup is scheduled work that follows an emergency. A repiping plan often begins with a burst pipe call.
