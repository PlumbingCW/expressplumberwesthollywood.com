Sewer backup emergency: what to do first

If sewage is rising, shut off water use in the unit and do not run the dishwasher, washer or shower. If it is already on the floor, avoid contact and open windows for air. Call us with where it is coming from and whether more than one fixture is involved.

A single fixture backing up points at a branch line. Multiple fixtures, especially the lowest ones, point at the main building line or the lateral. A camera inspection tells which one it is, and it often finds a crack, offset or root intrusion that explains why cabling did not hold.

How a failing lateral is found

The first step is a camera run through a cleanout or fixture. The camera shows cracks, offset joints, root intrusion, belly sections where water pools, and the transition from the building pipe to the lateral. A locator then marks the depth and path from above ground so a repair, if needed, is planned before anyone digs.

The camera record is more than a diagnosis. It is the document you need for permit review, for the property manager and for the insurer if backup damage occurred.

Cast iron, clay and ABS: what older West Hollywood lines are made of

The city rent stabilization ordinance applies to many multi-unit buildings with an original certificate of occupancy before July 1, 1979. Plumbing in buildings of that era can include cast iron stacks and clay or cast iron laterals. Cast iron rusts from the inside, which roughens the bore and catches debris, and clay joints let roots in.

Where the pipe is cast iron and failing, a section can be replaced with modern pipe and the transition made with proper couplings. We tell you honestly if a section is sound enough to keep and what warning signs to watch.

Private lateral and city main: who does what

The City of West Hollywood maintains its sewer mainlines, which connect to regional lines that carry sewage to the Hyperion Wastewater Treatment Plant in Playa del Rey. Property owners are responsible for operating and maintaining their private laterals, including the connection to the city main.

A plumber with a C-36 license can do work that stays on private property. Digging in the sidewalk, parkway or street needs a city encroachment permit and a contractor with an A or C-34 license and proof of insurance naming the city. If your repair reaches the public right-of-way, we tell you early.

Signs the line is failing and not just clogged

These patterns call for a camera look.

  • Repeated clogs in the same lineA cable removes the plug but not the cause.
  • Slow drains in several fixturesThe shared line is restricted.
  • Sewer smell indoors or in the yardA crack or open joint is venting gas or leaking.
  • Soggy or greener patches over the lineWater or sewage is leaking into the soil.
  • Trees or large shrubs near the lateralRoots enter at joints and cracks.

What shapes the cost of sewer repair

Quotes follow a camera inspection and are in writing.

Method

Spot repair, lining, bursting and trench work are priced very differently.

Depth and length

Deeper and longer repairs take more time and more excavation.

Surface restoration

Concrete, landscape or paving removed and replaced adds cost.

Permits

Work in the public right-of-way requires a city permit and insurance.

Access

A building with no cleanout may need one installed first.

Standard homeowner policies often exclude wear and root damage, and sewer backup coverage is an add-on. Ask your insurer before the work begins.
