New fixtures, moved lines and remodel rough-ins all begin with a plan. We handle plumbing installation in West Hollywood, CA for homes, condos, apartments and small commercial spaces. The work includes sinks, toilets, shower valves, water lines, drains, shutoffs and appliance connections.

This page explains how an installation job is scoped, when a permit is needed and how to avoid the common remodel surprises in older buildings.

What we install

Kitchen and bath fixtures, laundry connections, new supply lines, drain and vent changes, shutoff valves, pressure regulators, hose bibs and water heaters are the usual jobs. We work with fixtures you buy or with ones we supply. If you are choosing, we explain which brands have parts that remain available.

Permits in West Hollywood

The West Hollywood Plumbing Code requires a permit for installation, alteration, repair, replacement or remodel of a plumbing system. Repairs to leaks or the clearing of stoppages in drain, soil, waste or vent piping are exempt, as are repairs of leaks in water piping, valves or fixtures. Replacing that piping needs a permit.

We tell you at the quote stage whether your job needs a permit and who pulls it. The Building and Safety Division of the city handles the process, and inspections protect you if you sell or insure the property.

Remodels in older buildings

Buildings with an original certificate of occupancy before July 1, 1979 fall under West Hollywood rent stabilization, and many were built long before modern plumbing code. Opening a wall for a remodel can reveal galvanized supply lines, cast iron stacks or unvented drains. Each one changes the scope.

We walk the space before quoting and tell you which unknowns could change the price. For a larger remodel, we recommend a camera look at the drain and a pressure test of the supply, which can avoid a mid-project surprise.

Pressure, hardness and fixture choice

The California Plumbing Code calls for a pressure regulator when static pressure is above 80 psi, and we test before installing new fixtures. Hardness in Beverly Hills Water is about 290 mg/L, or roughly 17 grains per gallon, and hardness in LADWP supply varies by source. Hard water shortens the life of cartridges and aerators, so we suggest fixtures with serviceable parts.

If you are adding a dishwasher, ice maker or filter, we plan the connections together so each gets a proper shutoff.
