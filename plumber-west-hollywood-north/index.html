The area and its housing mix

A 2013 profile places West Hollywood North north of Santa Monica Boulevard and south of Sunset Boulevard, between San Vicente Boulevard and La Cienega Boulevard. The city's neighborhood watch list gives its member blocks, including parts of Holloway, West Knoll, Westbourne, Westmount, Hancock, Alta Loma, Cynthia, Palm and North San Vicente.

The same profile describes a mix of single-family homes, large apartment buildings and a few high-rises. Two documented historic groupings show the range. The Craftsman Historic District on Hancock and Palm Avenues, designated by the city in 1993, protects early 1910s bungalows. The city has also designated the original 1926 West Hollywood fire station on Palm Avenue, now a four-unit housing building.

Shared pipes and why they matter in multi-unit buildings

In a building with several units, a drain or supply line often serves more than one apartment. A clog that shows up in the lowest unit may start higher up in the building. If a neighbor has the same slow drain or a similar stain, tell the manager that more than one unit is affected. It is a stronger signal than one slow sink.

For a single-family bungalow, the questions are different. A wall leak is typically inside the walls of one house, and the repair route is leak detection followed by a targeted repair. See leak detection and drain cleaning for what those visits involve.

If sewage or a lot of water is coming up, do this first

General safety steps for any building type.

  • Stop using sinks, tubs and toilets that share the drain, since more water will make the backup worse.
  • Keep children and pets out of the area. Sewage water can carry bacteria, so wear gloves and avoid touching it.
  • Keep clear of standing water near outlets and appliances.
  • Tell the building manager if you rent, and take dated photos before cleanup for any insurance claim.

Permits for larger jobs

West Hollywood requires a permit for plumbing installation, alteration, repair, replacement and remodel work, with exemptions for leak repairs and clearing stoppages. Owners of bungalows and small buildings should ask whoever quotes a water heater swap or a drain relocation who will pull the permit.
