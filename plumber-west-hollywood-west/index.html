What West Hollywood West is

West Hollywood West covers the side streets north of Beverly Boulevard and south of Melrose Avenue, between Doheny Drive and La Cienega Boulevard. The city's neighborhood watch list names Almont, Doheny, Huntley, La Cienega, Norwich, Robertson, San Vicente, Sherbourne, Westbourne, West Knoll and Westmount among the streets in the residents association area, along with blocks of Beverly, Bonner, Dorrington, Melrose, Rangely and Rosewood.

A 2013 WEHOville profile reports almost 1,100 homes, calls it the only part of the city zoned exclusively for single-family homes and duplexes, and says the few apartment buildings predate the city's 1984 incorporation. It describes a neighborhood of long-term homeowners and many families. These figures are a decade old, so treat them as a description of character rather than a current count.

Why this differs from the rest of the city

About 80 percent of West Hollywood households rent and about 88 percent of its housing units are in multifamily buildings, according to the city's housing element data (ACS 2014 to 2018 and DOF 2020). West Hollywood West is described as the opposite pattern, with owner-occupied houses and duplexes. That changes who makes the decision when something fails.

In a house, the owner calls the plumber and pays for the repair. In a rented duplex unit, the tenant normally reports the problem to the landlord first.

Owner decisions are where the plumbing questions get interesting. Should a failing water heater be replaced with the same type, or is this the time to look at a tankless unit? Is the sewer line worth scanning before a remodel? Those are the questions below.

Before you buy or remodel here

  • Ask for a sewer camera scan if the sale or remodel depends on the line being sound.
  • Find the main shutoff and test it once. A valve that has not moved in years may not close fully.
  • Ask the seller or the previous contractor which permits were pulled for plumbing work. West Hollywood requires permits for most replacement and remodel plumbing.
  • Note where the sewer cleanout is. If it is buried or painted over, say so when you call.

Utilities for the area

Water in West Hollywood is mostly supplied by LADWP, with Beverly Hills Water serving some west-side addresses. Because West Hollywood West sits along the Beverly Hills edge, check your bill to see which utility serves your address. Sewer mains belong to the city, and the private line from your house to the main is the owner's responsibility.
