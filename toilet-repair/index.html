Toilet emergency: stop the overflow first

If the bowl is rising, remove the tank lid, lift the float or flapper chain and let it hang up. Close the supply valve on the wall. Do not flush again. The valve usually turns clockwise, and a stiff valve is a sign it needs replacing.

If water is coming from the floor around the base, close the valve and mop. Do not keep using a toilet that leaks at the base, because water can reach the subfloor and the unit below.

Clogs and when they point to a bigger problem

One-off clogs from paper or an object clear with a plunger or a closet auger. If a toilet clogs repeatedly, or if the tub gurgles when you flush, the blockage may be in the branch line or main. That moves the job from a toilet repair to drain cleaning.

We use a closet auger that protects the porcelain. We avoid chemical products, which damage seals and are unsafe to work around.

Running toilets and water bills

A flapper that does not seal lets water leak into the bowl all day. It can waste a surprising amount before anyone hears it. A dye tablet in the tank, without flushing, shows the leak within a few minutes if color appears in the bowl.

Parts in a tank cost little and the repair is quick. If the tank and bowl are old, cracked or stained, we explain when replacement makes more sense.

Rental, condo and building notes

West Hollywood is about 80 percent renters, by the census owner-occupied rate of 20.5 percent. If you rent, tell your manager in writing about a leaking or non-working toilet. Repair responsibility for plumbing fixtures generally sits with the owner unless the lease and law say otherwise.

In a condo, check whether the shutoff valve you are closing serves only your unit. A stuck or leaking valve that serves several units is a building issue.

Do not do these

Common mistakes make a small problem bigger.

  • Do not flush repeatedly on a clogWater has nowhere to go and spills on the floor.
  • Do not pour drain cleaner into a toiletIt can damage the finish and sits in the bowl for the plumber.
  • Do not force a stuck supply valveIt can snap and flood the room.
  • Do not keep using a toilet that rocksA loose base breaks the seal and leaks.
