Plumber in Center City, West Hollywood, CA

Center City is the most populated part of West Hollywood and is known for its 1920s and 1930s apartment buildings. Most people here rent, so a plumbing problem starts with two questions: is it inside your unit, and is it part of the building.

Call (213) 672-5590 if you own a unit, manage a building or have been asked to book a plumber. The sections below explain what to report, what to expect and how to handle parking and access in a dense area.

What Center City covers

A 2013 WEHOville profile describes Center City as running from Sunset Boulevard south to the city border and from La Cienega Boulevard east to Fairfax Avenue. It reports that about a fifth of the city's residents live there, which made it the most populous neighborhood. Residents described Fountain Avenue and Santa Monica Boulevard as busy, with quiet side streets and three small parks: Kings Road Park, Laurel Park and Hart Park.

The profile names large historic apartment buildings from the 1920s and 1930s, including La Fontaine, El Mirador and Villa Italia, mixed in with smaller apartment buildings, single-family homes and duplexes. The Preservation Alliance lists two documented districts here: the Courtyard Historic District, designated by the city in 1992, and the Harper Avenue National Register District, also from 1992, with period revival apartments arranged around courtyards.

What that means for you: in a dense building, one stoppage can affect several units, and a plumber needs the manager's access to shared lines, courtyard drains and basements. Tell the line whether you are the owner, the manager or a tenant when you call, since that decides who can approve the work.

Renters, landlords and building plumbing

About 80 percent of West Hollywood households rent and about 88 percent of its housing units are in multifamily buildings, according to the city's housing element data (ACS 2014 to 2018 and DOF 2020). No source in this research gives a renter share for Center City alone.

In general, plumbing inside the building walls and shared lines is the owner's or manager's responsibility, and tenants report problems in writing. The city's rent stabilization rules cover buildings with a certificate of occupancy before July 1, 1979. If you are unsure of your rights or duties, contact the city's rent stabilization division. This is general information, not legal advice.

What to tell the line when you call about an apartment

  • The building address, the unit number and the floor.
  • Whether neighbors above, below or beside you have the same problem.
  • Whether the water is clear, brown or smells like sewage.
  • Where a truck can stop. A 2013 profile called Center City's parking the tightest in the city because many older buildings have little or none.
  • Whether you can get access to a basement, courtyard drain or roof access, and who holds the key.
