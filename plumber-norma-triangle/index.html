Where Norma Triangle sits and what is documented

The city's neighborhood watch list places the Norma Triangle group between Doheny Drive on the west and San Vicente Boulevard on the east, with Vista Grande Street to the north and Santa Monica Boulevard to the south. Other published descriptions draw a wider triangle that reaches Sunset Boulevard and Holloway Drive, so the edges are not fixed. If your address is near a boundary, give the dispatcher your cross streets.

Reference sources say the first houses went up in the early 1900s for workers of the Los Angeles Pacific Railway and that many homes date to the 1920s. The housing is mostly one and two story houses in mixed styles. The city adopted Single-Family Design Guidelines for the area in 2017, which tells you the neighborhood is treated as a distinct single-family district.

Owning a small older house: general plumbing points

This section is general information, not a statement about any particular house here. A house that has stood for decades has usually had repairs, and those repairs may be mixed. Copper, galvanized steel, plastic and cast iron can all appear in one building, and the only way to know what you have is to look at it.

A camera inspection of the sewer line and a pressure check at the main shutoff will tell you more than the age of the house does.

If you are planning a remodel, ask what will happen to the supply lines and the drain in the walls you open. Opening a wall is the easiest moment to inspect pipe and to move or replace a valve. If you are buying, a sewer camera scan before closing is a common request because the line is buried and the repair is large if it fails.

Services homeowners here usually ask about

  • Drains and sewer line. Repeat clogs in a tub, floor drain or the main line. See drain cleaning for slow drains and sewer camera inspection for a look inside the line before you decide on repairs.
  • Water heaters. A tank that leaks or no longer heats. See water heater replacement. California requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped, and part of Norma Triangle sits inside the Hollywood Earthquake Fault Zone according to city and state mapping, so ask about strapping at the same visit.
  • Hidden leaks. A damp wall, a rising water bill or the sound of running water with every fixture off. See leak detection.
  • Supply line problems. Low pressure across the whole house can point to the main line or the pressure regulator. See water line repair.

Permits and who to check with

West Hollywood requires a permit for plumbing installation, alteration, repair, replacement and remodel work, and it exempts leak repairs and clearing stoppages. Ask whoever quotes your job whether a permit applies to it. If a quote involves new gas piping, a water heater swap or relocating a drain, assume the question is worth asking.

Water service in West Hollywood is mostly from LADWP, and some west-side addresses are served by Beverly Hills Water. Your bill shows which utility serves your address.
