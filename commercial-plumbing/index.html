Emergencies that stop a business

A grease-clogged kitchen line, a failed water heater, a burst supply line, a toilet that overflows in a public restroom and a sewer backup into a floor drain are the usual causes of closing. The faster we know the problem, the faster we arrive prepared.

Tell us the type of business, the building floor, what is affected and whether other tenants are involved. In a multi-tenant building, we ask who holds the building plumbing, because a shared stack changes who approves the work.

Restaurants, grease and drain schedules

West Hollywood has a dense restaurant and nightlife corridor along the Sunset Strip and Santa Monica Boulevard. Kitchens produce grease, starch and food solids that settle in horizontal drain runs. A line that is jetted on a schedule stays clear far longer than a line that is cabled only after it blocks.

We can recommend an interval after seeing the line, and we record camera footage so you have a baseline. A grease trap or interceptor needs its own service, and we tell you when that belongs to a hauler.

Water heaters and hot water in commercial buildings

A single heater serving a building, a tankless bank or a recirculation loop each have different failure patterns. A failed heater in an apartment building affects every unit at once, and tenants expect a fast answer. We service tank and tankless systems and can plan a replacement during off hours.

For design and sizing questions, see water heater replacement and tankless water heater for the details we cover with owners.

Permits, licenses and the property manager

The West Hollywood Plumbing Code requires a permit for installation, alteration, repair, replacement or remodel of plumbing systems, with exemptions for leak repair and stoppage clearing. We explain which your job needs. Work on the private lateral can be done by a C-36 plumber, and work in the public right-of-way needs an encroachment permit and a contractor with the proper license class.

We provide written reports with photos, the work done and the cause found, which managers use for owners, tenants and insurers. Verify any contractor license on the state board site before hiring, as the state requires a license for jobs above the state labor-and-materials threshold.
