Disposal emergency: safe first steps when it jams

Switch the disposal off at the wall. Never put a hand in the chamber, even when it is off. Use tongs or pliers to remove an object you can see. Many disposals have a hex socket in the bottom that lets you turn the flywheel by hand with an allen key, and a red reset button on the underside.

If water backs up from the disposal into the sink, stop running water, as it can overflow. That is a drain issue, not just a disposal issue.

Repair or replace

A jam or a tripped reset is a short repair. A leak from the body means a failed seal, and the disposal is replaced. If the unit is more than a decade old, or if it has jammed repeatedly, replacement usually costs less over time than repair.

We install common units from a modest model for light use to a larger one for heavy use. We check the dishwasher drain connection, the electrical connection and the trap during the work.

What really harms a disposal

Fibrous food like celery, corn husks and onion skins, coffee grounds in large amounts, pasta and rice that swell and grease all cause clogs downstream. Bones and fruit pits can jam the unit. Run cold water before, during and after use so grease moves through the line.

Kitchens in older West Hollywood buildings often have long, shared branch lines. A disposal puts food waste into a line that was designed with less volume in mind. If you live in a building with chronic kitchen clogs, ask the manager about a scheduled jet.

Rentals, condos and who handles it

Many West Hollywood rentals come with a disposal. If it fails, tell the owner or manager in writing, since the unit is typically part of the appliance package the landlord provides. In condos, check the HOA rules for under-sink modifications before replacing.

We can work with a manager to approve and bill the work, and we leave a written note of what was done.

Habits that extend a disposal

Small changes help.

  • Use cold water while running itCold water keeps grease solid so it moves along.
  • Cut large scraps firstSmaller pieces are easier on the unit.
  • Avoid fibrous and starchy foodsThey wrap and swell.
  • Clean with ice and citrus peel only occasionallyIt freshens but does not replace cleaning the line.

What not to do with a jammed disposal

A jam is easy to make worse.

  • Do not put your hand in the chamberEven a switched-off unit can turn if the flywheel moves.
  • Do not pour drain cleaner into itCaustic liquid sits in the unit and is a hazard for whoever opens it.
  • Do not keep pressing the switchA humming motor overheats and trips the reset.
  • Do not run water into a backed-up sinkIt can overflow onto the counter and floor.
